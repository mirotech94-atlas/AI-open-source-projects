ATLAS browser collector

The deny-list below never leaves this browser. Pages it matches are not sent, not buffered, and not written to disk. ATLAS has no copy of it.

Where to send

Must be your ATLAS mesh address. This endpoint is not reachable from the public internet, by design — if it works off the mesh, something is wrong.
The value of BROWSER_INGEST_TOKEN in your ATLAS .env.

What to send

Unchecked, ATLAS receives only the domain, the time and how long you spent. That is enough for the patterns it reports on, and much less to hold.
Server-side, raw history is deleted after BROWSER_RETENTION_DAYS; the per-domain roll-up survives.

Deny-list

A line with a dot matches that domain and its subdomains (chase.com also blocks secure.chase.com). A bare word matches any domain containing it as a label — bank blocks mybank.co.uk.
