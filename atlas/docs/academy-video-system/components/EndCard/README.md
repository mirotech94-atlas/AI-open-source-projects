The 15-second end card: the skill just learned, one specific next lesson, and a secondary shop line, with the right 640px (`endscreen-reserve`) left empty for YouTube's native next-video element.

**The editor supplies:** the result sentence, the exact title of an existing next video, one line saying why it follows, and (only when relevant) the matching course or practice-files name. The real end-screen element is added in Studio and previewed.

**Behaviour:** the card fades in with `title-rise` at 15 s from the end and stays until the last frame; the narration finishes its sentence and the audio resolves. Nothing fades away early; no "goodbye" scene follows it.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Point to one specific available video | Offer three choices or "check out my channel" |
| Keep the reserve empty in the export | Paint a fake thumbnail or a drawn subscribe button there |
| Keep the shop line secondary and the link in the description | Turn the ending into a pitch on every episode |
