The result-first opening: the finished outcome on screen in the first three seconds, with one `promise`-size badge that names it. It replaces any logo sequence or welcome paragraph.

**The editor supplies:** the real recorded result (both panes are real app captures, not mockups), the badge text (three words or fewer, sentence case), and the timecode the badge fades in (frame 12, after the app is readable).

**Behaviour:** the badge enters with `title-rise` on `settle`, bottom-left inside `safe-16-9`, and stays until the trace begins at 3 s. The badge fill is `success` only when the outcome really is a tested, review-ready result; otherwise use a `navy-raised` plate with `paper` text.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Show the same outcome the title and thumbnail promise | Open on a logo, a face card or a "welcome to the channel" line |
| Keep one headline on the frame | Stack a caption, a second line and an animated icon on top of it |
| Match the badge wording to what the workflow enforces ("Ready for review" only if review is required) | Imply a safeguard that the demo does not show |
