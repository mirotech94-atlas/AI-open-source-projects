# Pre-publish checklist

Run it on the exported file, on a phone, with sound. Every row is pass/fail; one fail sends the episode back a stage.

## Promise

- Title, thumbnail and the first 3 seconds show the same visible outcome.
- The result is on screen before the sting; the sting is 1 second or less.
- The reusable opening line names the outcome, the scope, the success condition and the first action.

## Readability

- Nothing on a 16:9 frame is smaller than `support` (28px); nothing on a Short is smaller than `shorts-caption` (60px).
- Key code and values are read aloud and held for at least `hold-tag`.
- Every label sits inside the safe area for its format.
- Captions sit on their plate and never cover a control or a result.

## Motion

- One moving focal point at a time; no transitions on a timer; no looping background motion.
- Each effect is one of the system's presets at its `duration` token.
- The cursor ring pulses once per meaningful click and then stops.
- Software-state changes are cuts, not crossfades.

## Accuracy

- Every `success` chip corresponds to a test that actually passed on camera.
- Omitted waiting time is labelled.
- Prerequisites, accounts and costs are stated accurately.
- No animation implies an integration or result that was not demonstrated.

## Audio and captions

- Speech is intelligible with music ducked under explanations; no voice-distorting processing.
- Fades do not cut words.
- Technical names, node names and links are spelled as the product spells them.

## Continuation

- The next lesson named on the end card exists and is specific.
- The native end-screen element is added in Studio and previewed; the right 640px of the end card are empty in the export.
- The description carries the exact resource link with an accurate one-line explanation; no sales pitch in the lesson body.

## The four-video test

Introduce the result-first opening on two similar tutorials; keep it and add workflow/checkpoint motion on the next two. Record the change dates, compare equal 7-day and 28-day windows, and read first-30-second retention, drops at the sting and explanation cards, average view duration, end-screen clicks and comments about clarity. Keep what improved clarity; remove what distracted.
